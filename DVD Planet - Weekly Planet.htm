volume 1 - issue 28
december 2000
If gunfights are your thing, you're in luck this week with the release of Shaft, a hip new spin on the blaxploitation favorite, and the long awaited first season of The Sopranos. (And be sure to check out our in-depth review of this spectacular set.) If that's too sedate, prepare yourself for the surprise gross out hit of the year, Scary Movie, complete with even more stuff they didn't show you in the theater. Of course, be sure to also stick around all the way through the end credits. Finally, for the more wholesome crowd, DreamWorks' lavish entry in the animation sweepstakes, The Road to El Dorado, arrives hot on the heels of their other groundbreaking animated yarns. While that should be enough to tide you over for a week, don't forget to look at some of the big titles announced recently for the shiny disc, including the sleepr horror hit,What Lies Beneath, guaranteed to have nervous housewives and scoundrel husbands squirming in fear.



It's never too early to plan for the holidays, and we've decided to help you go ahead and pick out those DVD stocking stuffers with our Christmas on DVD spotlight. From movies to music to cartoons, every holiday favorite is right here to get you in a festive spirit. Or for gift suggestions of a more unusual nature, take a look at DVD Dementia from Frank Henenlotter, who takes a quirky and often uproarious look at newly released cult favorites from the likes of Lucio Fulci and Andy Milligan. On the review front we've got a slew of new additions, including Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer, The Year without a Santa Claus,, Four Times That Night, Hockney at the Tate, Nunsense 2, The Blob, both volumes of The Prisoner, The Stuff, Horrors of Spider Island, After Life, Evil Dead Trap, and Pitch Black. We're also p to announce the addition of reviews from Stu Kobak's Films on Disc to DVDPlanet, so browse around and enjoy his take on a wide range of titles like American Beauty, A Star Is Born, The Perfect Storm, Airplane!, and many others.


- Nathaniel Thompson, Editor
