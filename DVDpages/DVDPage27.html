Tiger of Wanchai/C,C+

Universe/1994/9m

     Right smack dab in the middle of this Triad flick, a montage segment inserted that plays like a music video that might have been called "Triad's Rising Star." It even reminded me of some sections of Evita. Though well done, it saps the energy from "Tiger," and is indicative of a certain lack of direction which plagues the film.    

.©Universal

Don't expect too much from this Hong Kong transfer. It's adequate in sharpness, though several scenes are less than optimal and colors are not tightly focused.

 

 

 

 

Operation Condor:Armor of God II/C+,B-

Media Asia/1997/125m

     This is Jackie Chan in full comic throttle. He's a manic mix of those silent movie stars of yesteryear. Think Harold Lloyd doing impossible stunts, think the entire array of Keystone Cops in antic chase. The climax that finds Jackie blowing in a wind tunnel is quite funny, though perhaps it goes on to long. It's the purest Chan sight gag I have viewed and I can understand the director's reluctance for shorten the blasts of wind from this sequence.

Jackie and friends©Media Asia

     In some ways this is a rip-off of Raiders of the Lost Ark
     The widescreen images on this import DVD are pretty good. There is some excessive enhancement which causes some NTSC artifacting, and some scenes lack tight color.
