S.D.U. '97/C,C+

New City/1997/89m

     A Hong Kong action flick that plays like pure John Woo wannabe, but lacks the focus and discipline contained in the best of Woo like The Killer and even the lesser Hard Boiled.

The SDU crew ready to go into action.©New City

     During a major special drug undercover operation, several cops are made fall guys for the mistake of an ambitious superior. They leave the force and become owners of a bar and B-girl operation. As their lives stay afloat  trying to succeed in business, they are plagued by empty cash registers, missed romantic opportunities, and too much drinking. Director Cho Wing tries to inject some feeling of angst into the developments, but as their lives drag so does the film.
     The action picks up when the former SDU team members are contacted to find the kidnapped daughter of an industrialist. They travel to another city , assemble the team and bullets and kicks fly as they recover the girl. None of it is done with much panache, the stunts are minor league and the star personalities do not outshine the material.
     This Hong Kong import DVD is presented widescreen with English and Chinese subtitle on the print. The image is reasonably sharp. Contrast levels are inconsistent, sometimes giving the film a washed out look. Still, the mediocre image doesn't stand in the way of enjoying the film. It's the script! There are obvious compression artifacts. The Dolby Digital 2-channel sound is all right, but it's not a great DVD mix.
