Alien/A,B+
DVD Review

 

It's a classic. A perfect marriage of two film genres, Science Fiction and Horror.

It's a great treat to watch an anamorphic transfer of this great movie in my home theater. The image is very strong throughout with Scott's intelligent camera providing sharp detail on DVD.  Some scenes with extended running are slightly soft, but this is likely the way the original material looks.

The Dolby Digital 5:1 mix is rather undernourished in output. The information is plenty spatial, but dialogue seems too timid. I cranked it up a good five decibels before feeling comfortable. To be just, some of the dynamic range is clearly intentional and in fact moving from quiet to loud in short bursts is highly effective for a Horror movie.
