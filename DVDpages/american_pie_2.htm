American Pie 2 (SE)/ C,B
Universal/2001/

     Baking pie from a recipe should deliver the same results every time, right. American Pie 2 uses the same ingredients for a less successful variation of the recipe. It takes far too long for any laughs to build and it depends heavily on familiarity with the original American Pie for much of its humor. Lord help you if you didn't see the first. The big question is: Who took the pie out of American Pie 2?  Alternate titles might have been: American Pie Goes to the Beach, Beach Blanket Pie, The Summer I Lost My Cherry Pie, Can You Bake A Cherry Pie Charming Jim?

 

©

    American Pie 2 is slightly less robust than its predecessor. The image is consistently sharp, but overall light output could have been more powerful. Colors are very warm and flesh tones tend to go pasty. A choice of DTS and Dolby Digital 5:1 surrounds each deliver acceptably active sound tracks, with DTS typically 5 dB up from Dolby.
