   Angel Eyes gets  a stunning transfer in this special edition DVD featuring an audio commentary from director Luis Mandoki. There's no peak transition ringing or enhancement in evidence. Resolution range is excellent without producing an edgy look. This really looks like film. Colors have a lovely, full, subtle range. Lighting is preserved with meticulous care. Night scenes sparkle. Blacks are delivered with outstanding polish. It's a very nice Dolby Digital 5:1 surround mix. Good location of ambient details and music that floats over the action.
