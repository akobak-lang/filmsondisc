Apostle, The/B+,B+

When one of the great stars of his generation gets behind the camera, savvy moviegoers take a peek. With The Apostle, his second directing effort, Robert Duval brings the kind of casual intensity that framed so many spectacular screen performances. The Apostle, written and directed by Duval, is storytelling, talking about the preacher man who dominated so many lives in this town or that, it's about a way of life and where a life came from.

In a few simple cinematic strokes, Duval glimpses the origins of Sonny Dewey, The Apostle of the title.

Sonny Dewey is a character with such cosmic vitality, it's a shame to see his downfall.  The truth is that what's so great about Sonny is also what destroys him. The mercurial nature of the revivalist spirit is the same spirit that Sonny brings to everyday life.  The fact that he can't control it destroys him.
