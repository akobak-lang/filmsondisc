As Good As it Gets/B+,AD
DVD Review

 

Basking in its own eccentricity, As Get as it Gets, succeeds on the strength of its lead performances more than anything else. Character is the name of the game in James Brooks' screenplay and Marvin is one hell of a character. Jack Nicholson does his best screen work in years, taking every Marvin quirk to the extreme and multiplying its effectiveness by digging at the core of the tic.

     This is an absolutely beautiful transfer. It's not a showy film with special effects to show off the merits of the DVD medium, but the extraordinary film-like seamlessness of the DVD should not be overlooked. Colors are vibrant, perfectly controlled; yet disappear into the overall palette of As Good as it Gets. The night sequences glisten entertainingly and the interiors capture a perfect balance of lighting. Sonically, this is an easy-going disc, producing dialogue to perfection and surrounding the characters with an accurate ambient of road noise on their trip to Maryland.
