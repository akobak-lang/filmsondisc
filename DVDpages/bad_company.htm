Bad Company
DVD Review

Robert Benton's lean look at youth on the road heaing West hoping to pave a way to new lives after the upheaval of the Civil War. Jeff Bridges captures the amoral flavor of Jake Rumsey with perspicacious clarity. If there's a weak link to the film Barry Brown's bland performance as Drew Dixon.  He's okay, but his character fails to come to life with the same intensity as Bridges, more showy character.
