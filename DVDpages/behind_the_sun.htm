Behind the Sun/ A-, A-
DVD Review

I saw another beautifully directed film from Salles, Behind the Sun. It’s set in rural Brazil at the turn of the century. A family feud dominates this coming of age picture. There’s purity to Behind the Sun that is frequently absent from films. The hate is portrayed with frightening paralytic power. It positively takes the heart from two feuding families. There’s a twisted sense of honor, but that is often a part of feuds, rational or irrational. Salles films Behind the Sun with imaginative beauty. It emphasizes aspects of the purity. The characters, the setting, the simple story, resonate with passionate filmmaking. I highly recommend this one!

Another film that I highly recommend is Behind the Sun, a film by Walter Salles (Central Station). There’s plenty of passion in Behind the Sun, but its perverted by hate and blinded by the sun. It’s the story of two feuding families in rural Brazil around the turn of the century. Beautifully filmed with some rare artistic compositions that express the characters feelings to perfection.
