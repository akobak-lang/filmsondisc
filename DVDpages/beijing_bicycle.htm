Beijing Bicycle
Columbia/2001/113/

     It's a simnplistic vision of country boy coming to the city and facing a cold welcome. It also an excellent contrast of the new relatively priviliged Chinese middle class and the hard working no-nonsense ethic of the country folk. Realistically, the film could have been a half hour short. There's lotts of repetition. Yes, it's controlled repetition, bunt it puts a heavy drag on the simple narrative.

You have to admire the grit of young Gue. Rarely has a character in a film exhibited such blind determination. That's also part of the weakness of the film. Sometimes it abandons logic in favor  the single-minded vision of filmmaker Xiaoshuai Wang.  Lin Cui is very good as Gue. He's innocent, slightly dense, and petals his character with consistent energy.

    It's a good, solid transfer. Some slight edge enhancement and a few scenes are less than optimally sharp. Color is fine. Yellow English subtitles are clear and easy to read.
