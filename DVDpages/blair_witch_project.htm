Blair Witch Project(SE)/B,B+

Columbia/1999/124m/ANA 1.85

     The tag lines are endless: three novice filmmakers disappear into their own film; lost film found but no trace of filmmakers; low budget witches brew. Most important, The Blair Witch Project is horrifying in its execution. There’s plenty wrong with this film. It plays slowly and repetitiously; you could even call it boring, but it plays with fear, it builds on fear, and its roots are the purest form of horror.

Is that it? ©New Line
     Accompanying the film on this special edition from Artisan is a nifty promotional mocumentary called The Curse of the Blair Witch. I think I might have enjoyed the film more if I had watched this first.
     The image is consistently clean with excellent contrast. Some of the night scenes compress detail to some extent, but it works well for the film and is likely the way it is intended to be seen.
