Born on the Fourth of July/
DVD Review

 

©

      Sad to say, Born on the Fourth of July is a travesty of jumpy edges and stair-step diagonal lines. It appears that the transfer was made from either a composite master or three-two pulldown was not encoded. Whichever, the result is not very watchable. Even the color is rather washed out and pasty. Some images are soft. The Dolby Digital 2 channel surround sound is all right.
    It's ironic that Oliver Stone's fine audio commentary appears making this a special edition. Diehards may want the DVD simply for the Stone observations. Stone, an articulate and knowledgeable speaker about politics and filmmaking remembers production details with confident clarity.
