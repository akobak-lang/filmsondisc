Boxcar Bertha/ B, B
MGM/1972/88/ANA 1.85

Barbara Hershey is such a fresh face in Boxcar Bertha. The bloom of youth radiates from her. She has great screen energy. 

Even early in his career director Scorsese really knows where to place the camera. He possesses a wonderful film vocabulary.

Seeing David Carradine as Big Bill you cant help thinking about his later starring road as Woody Guthrie in Bound for Glory. The character is even slightly reminescent of his portrayal in that other dust bowl union flick.

There are elements of Bonnie and Clyde in the free-spirited quartet of companions. Bernie Casey and Barry Primus are effective  Von and Rafe. 

Riding the rails.  ©MGM

     Its a pretty good transfer, but resolution is clearly less than optimal. Color saturation is very nice and grainier sequences are delivered in tight even patterns. The elements are very clean. The thirties folk and blues tracks featuring harmonica and guitar sound play very nicely on the mono soundtrack. Black level is quite good on the tuxedo scene with revealing details.
