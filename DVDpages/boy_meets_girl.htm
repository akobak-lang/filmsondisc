Boy Meets Girl/B+,B
DVD Review

    Leos Carax seems so close to his material in Boy Meets Girl. The spare, very visual, screenplay is both internal and external at the same time. Dialogue comes in fitful bursts, outpouring of emotion, while the internal turmoil of the characters simmers under the surface of the key lit dramatic black and white photography.
