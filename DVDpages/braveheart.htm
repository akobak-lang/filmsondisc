Braveheart (SE)/B+,A
Paramount/1995/

      Two actor/directors brought back the epic screen vision in the 1990s. Kevin Costner scored with his three hour western Dances with Wolves and Mel Gibson brought the sweep of Scottish rebellion to life with Braveheart. Gibson's film is breathtakingly made, with sweeping battles, brutally depicted, and romantic moments accomplished with equal style.

     Audio commentary by actor director Mel Gibson is a special treat. This is a long movie and Gibson does not fill every moment with filmmaking insights, but after the first hour, he warms to the task of talking comfortably about the film and his comments are often on target and interesting. Gibson tries to give an honest perspective, noting questionable historical liberties. The Making of Braveheart: A Filmmaker's Passion provides a nice view of the filmmaking process with an interesting behind the scenes look at the principals on location.
