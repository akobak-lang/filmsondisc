Chocolat (SE)/C,C+
Miramax/2000/122/ANA 1.85

    I wish I could say Chocolat enchanted me. The players, the setting, the main ingredient all have appeal, but I felt left in the dark watching the film in the darkness of the home theater. I was bored. Not charmed. Much of the film was so mean spirited. And worst of all, the chocolates, the chocolate preparations, seemed mundane.
    Even one of my favorite actresses, Juliet Binoche, seemed like she'd been chewing on too many choice chocolates. Her smile seemed a touch forced and did I detect little bits of brown evidence in the corners of her lush mouth?

 

 

©

    The DVD transfer contains far too many soft scenes in this fairy tale concoction. It's quite possible that director Halstram was liberally filtered the film, but it did not seem that was the reason for the bandwidth roll-off. The colors are often delicate, again fitting in with the director's intent. Saturation is good enough. Can you hear the North wind blowing? Yes, the Dolby Digital 5:1 surround information provides the right ambiance to enjoy the would-be charm of the film.
