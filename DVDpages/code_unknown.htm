Code Unknown/ B+, D
DVD Review

    There's no denying the power of Michael Haneke's fractured yet compelling film Code Unknown. Interweaving fragments from the lives of a number of characters with dramatic, abrupt, black-out cuts, Haneke's daring style is jarring, involving, and refreshing. Just how closely the stories intertwine may not be obvious, yet, each segment gets significant weight and stands on its own while blending in with Haneke's master plan.

     Juliette Binoche glows. The actress glows even more without the intrusion of artifice. Anne is a natural, earthy character, centered around Binoche's own screen force.
     Too bad Code Unknown gets trashy treatment on DVD. The image is extremely soft throughout. Viewers are not able to perceive some of the character thought processes by keen observation of characters' eyes.
