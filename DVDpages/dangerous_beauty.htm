Dangerous Beauty/ A-,A-
DVD Review

 

Look out for a film called Courtesan. It's a

beauty set in 15th Century Venice. A beautiful evocation of the period

on a limited budget. Catherine MacCormack who played the lovely

sweetheart in Braveheart stars. Directed by Marshall Herskovitz(Jack the

Bear). It will probably have a different name when released.

 

A splendid spirit of feminine independent permeates Dangerous Beauty. Hershkovitz paints a marvelous picture of Venice with the camera and while some budget restraints are evident(If 50,000 people succumbed to the plague how come Venice was always pretty empty.), the setting is majestic.
