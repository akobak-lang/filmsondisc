Death Wish
DVD Review

    Death Wish may be a simplistic take on making a stand against turning your back on crime. The fact that the film spawned four sequels is a testament to the popularity of the message. The first time out, there's no denying the power of Death Wish. Director Michael Winner effectively depicts the horror and then coolly lets the hero play out his hand, ever more steadily. Making inspector Ochoa a comic presence gives the film an excellent balance. Winner knows enough to keep the comedy reigned in

These guys picked the wrong car. ©Paramount

     A solid transfer from good film elements, Death Wish is consistently sharp. The many night scenes glitter with the edge of key lit gloss, providing excellent shadow detail.
