Drunken Angel

Mei Ah/1947/97m/BW 1.33

     The beginning of a magnificent collaboration, Drunken Angel is the first of the Kurosawa films starring Toshiro Mifune. No, he's not the familiar samurai, but a gangster, tough, young, bitter, and beautiful.

Tough gangster, tough doctor. ©Mei Ah
