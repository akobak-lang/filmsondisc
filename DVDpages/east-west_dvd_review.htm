 East/West (SE)/B+, C+
Columbia/1999/

    The idealist sometimes gets a dose of reality never to be forgotten. At the end of World War II, a large number of French expatriate communists decided to return to Mother Russia. Some, like Very mature, penetrating material. It's the same director who did Indochine, but it's much more controlled and involving. It's not beautiful to look at, that's for sure.

    A washed out, grim looking DVD. Some scenes rather soft. Now, I think the grayish tones may have been the intent of the director. The sun really does not shine on these people. Make wrong move, as dramatic as these people did, and life can turn around on a dime. The director and writer provide audio commentary. Their comments range from technical to political. Conversational, relaxed and good company. I wouldn't mind discussing films with these guys.
