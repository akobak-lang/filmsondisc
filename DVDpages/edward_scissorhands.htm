Edward Scissorhands (SE)/B+,B

     Tim Burton creates roller coaster rides of movies, toppling genre conventions and investing them with an imaginative, often skewed sensibility. The undulations of a Burton vision often provide unique entertainment. Edward Scissorhands is one of his best. Burton takes on the Frankenstein story and turns it into an urban fable. With wonderfully evocative droll sets, hairdos to die for, and dialogue

Show and tell the Burton way. ©Columbia
