Eight Men Out/B,C-
MGM/1988/120/ANA 1.85

    There's a lot of ice in the storytelling of the 1919 World Series scandal. Eight Men Out works best when it tugs at the emotional heart strings.

    The transfer is so disappointing. Considering it's anamorphic, I thought Eight Men Out would look like a baseball game in full summer stride. Instead it's a rainy spring for the film. Colors are somewhat washed out and the image is far too soft. Contrast level is less than optimal.
