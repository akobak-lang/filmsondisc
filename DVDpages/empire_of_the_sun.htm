Empire of the Sun (SE) /A, B+
Warner/1987/152/ANA 1.85

 

The first fifty minutes is perhaps the best filmmaking I have ever seen. The film is clearly Spielberg’s best. The raw material is so powerful that even when Spielberg reaches into his bag of sentiment for one of those magic moments, it is tempered by the setting and works very well. Christian Bale gives one of the all-time great kid performances. It’s interesting to note Spielberg’s directions to him during the accompanying documentary, which is very good indeed. Excellent looking DVD; it catches the mood very well. I highly recommend getting the DVD.

    The script by Tom Stoppard is very sophisticated. It draws heavily on the spare, elegant original biographical novel by J. G. Ballard. 

   The John Williams core soars. The bucolic voices of the young boys choir underscores the horrors beyond perfection.
