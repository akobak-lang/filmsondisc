     The End of the Affair is such a moody and melancholy film, it looks as if it exists in a land where the sun never shines. The lugubrious action is accented by several explosions that tend to wake viewers up, but there's little else that might inspire excitement.

     From one of Grahame Greene's "catholic" novels, the affair in question takes place between a writer and unfulfilled wife of a cabinet minister in war time London.
