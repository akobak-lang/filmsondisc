Entre Nous
DVD Review

 1983 112m WS 2.35

 

A period film transferred with spit and polish, Entre Nous is one of the better entries from the foreign film specialists, Fox Lorber. The colors are truly vital capturing the rich vision of the past filtered through director Diane Kurys eyes. Some slight enhancement has been used to emphasize sharpness, but it’s not unsightly. Contrast is deep with velvety blacks and easy transitions from bright to dim light. English titles are removable and are set into the black border of the letterboxed image. Menu information is limited with filmographies for the two stars and director. Some production notes for this fine French film would have made an interesting addition. The sound field is more than adequate to convey the place and mood. Entre Nous is a beautiful film that will touch you through its honesty.
