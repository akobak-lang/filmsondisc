Flamingo Kid, The/B+,C
Anchor Bay/19

 

     You certainly have to admire any father whose favorite restaurant's proudly announces the motto "Any Fish You Wish." That says a lot about the sweetness behind the execution of The Flamingo Kid. Ultimately, this is a film about family.
