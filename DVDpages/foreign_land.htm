Foreign Land/ B+, D+
DVD Review

, I watched an excellent Brazilian film last night that began with Brazilian financial difficulties as a jumping off point. Foreign Land turned out to be an excellent offbeat Noirish kind of film. It’s directed by Walter Salles and Daniela Thomas. I am not certain why the focus on the 1990 freeze on bank accounts and conversion to cruizeros. It doesn’t seem to fit with the rest of the plot, though it’s posed as the reason why so many young Brazilians leave the country, as the protagonist winds up doing. Its set primarily in Portugal, though the intercutting with the early San Paulo scenes is excellent. You know that these parallel stories are going to collide, but you’re not sure how it will happen. I like the characters very much. I recommend it if you have already seen it. It’s in black and white and the source elements are not very good. Extremely grainy, dirt and scratches to boot, yet this compelling little film never let my attention waiver.
