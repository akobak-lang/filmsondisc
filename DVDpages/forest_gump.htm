Forest Gump (SE)/A,A-
DVD Review

Last night it was Forest Gump. Man, this film gets better every time I watch it. Hanks is nothing short of amazing. Is there a misstep in this performance? I don't think so. Zemeckis's sweeping canvas covering twenty
years of our history through the movements of Forest is brilliantly executed. Forest Gump is a paean to the innocence of America, through all sorts of tragedies, through war, assassination, hate, our innocence survives through Forest, through his simple belief in humanity. The DVD looks terrific. Colors jump out at you. Every look in Forest's eyes is crystal clear. The fire and smoke Sinese's Lieutenant Dan is captured in all its
intensity. I have to go through some of the extras and expand my knowledge of chocolates.
