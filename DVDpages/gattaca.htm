DVD Review

Gattaca

A friend I have a great deal of respect for called Gattaca "thinking man's science fiction."  It's true that this is not a film dominated by special effects and action and that the pacing is thoughtful and deliberate. While there are interesting elements in the world writer/director Andrew Niccol has created for Gattaca, this vision related to 1984 does not come off with vitality. Part of the problem with the film is the metronomic pacing. There's a droning sense to Gattaca

Gattaca has been turned into a fine looking DVD. There are some slightly fuzzy scenes, but this is due more to the shooting and lighting style than the transfer effort.
