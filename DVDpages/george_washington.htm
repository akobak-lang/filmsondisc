George Washington (SE)/ B+, B+
Criterion/2001/

    This is a very graceful production, from the sensitive script to the fluid camera movement to the touching performances.

    The relaxed and casual pacing of the film succeeds in capturing a feeling of place. There are lots of two-person rambling conversations which illuminate the world of George Washington.

    The children are quite lovely, albeit damaged in different ways. Some of the dialogue provides very adult insights filtered through childhood's innocence.

The story telling is free-form, like a sense memory piece. TYhe material is very touching. The picture of small town life in an economically depressed area is consistently moving.
