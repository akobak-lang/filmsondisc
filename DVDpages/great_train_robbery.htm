Great Train Robbery (SE)/B,B
DVD Review

     Looking for an entertainment crafted with genuine pleasure? The Great Train Robbery fits the bill. Michael Crichton's (Jurassic Park) novel has been adapted to the screen by the novelist for his directing debut. It was quite a lot of fun. Sean Connery as the leader of a group making the first heist of a moving train circa 1850s London. Sexy Leslie Ann Down dresses her curvaceous body with lots of good humor. Audio commentary by writer director Michael Crichton is pretty good from the little I listened. I like his observation that Sean Connery eats on screen better than any actor he knows. I will forever watch every chew made by 007.
Appraising Down's assets. ©MGM

    Though maximum resolution has not been extracted from the source through an anamorphic transfer, the DVD looks very good. Most scenes are sharper than you'd expect.
