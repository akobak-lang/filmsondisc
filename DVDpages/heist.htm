Warner/2001/109/ANA 1.85

    "Everyone wants money. That's why they call it money!" "She could talk her way out of a sunburn."

Hackman is a great marriage for the terse, clipped Mamet dialogue. He grinds off some of the edge of unreality, making it live with glimmer of warmth. He breaks it off into bites size pieces of perfectly baked bagel.

Joe and company are more like conman than heistmaster. The heists are good, but the heart of the film is still more con. 

More con than heist flick. ©Warner

    Absolutely terrific score. Well recorded, interesting variety of motifs.

 

Bright transfer. Good shadow detail.
