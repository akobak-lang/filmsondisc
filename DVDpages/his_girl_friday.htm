His Girl Friday/
Columbia/1940/92/BW/FS 1.33

 

©

This news staff is quickly turning into a male fraternal order. Have no fear and hold your sides because Rosalind Russell is the perfect cure for taking this venture too seriously. Russell is breathtakingly hyper and startlingly stunning as reporter Hildy Johnson in Howard Hawk's His Girl Friday, the  smashing remake of The Front Page. Russell stakes claim to the madcap crown of frenetic journalism. Russell's energy will keep the news room fast and loose and despite getting her job on the strength of this comic tour-de-force, she'll prove she can go out and get the story with the best of them.

            Along for the ride on this roller coaster of hilarity is Cary Grant, matching Russell's breathless banter barb for wit. His Girl Friday was Grant's one newspaper role and he acquits himself wonderfully as Hildy's editor, Walter Burns; nevertheless, I will pass on the great temptation of finding a place for Grant on the staff.
