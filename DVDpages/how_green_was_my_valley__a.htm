How Green Was My Valley  A, B+
Fox /1941/118 minutes/FS 1.33

        Absolutely splendid emotionally involving story of  proud family of Welsh coal minors in turmoil.  Ford's crisp, straightforward directing style is complimented by exemplary ensemble acting. Each actor inhabits his role to perfection.  Donald Crisp is incredibly powerful in the role of the father and Roddy McDowall gives an unaffected child performance. Walter Pidgeon's Minister Gruffydd is beautifully controlled and Maureen O'Hara has never been more effective or beautiful.

        Arthur C. Miller won the Academy Award for Best Cinematography(Black and White)for capturing so clearly the beauty and  dignity of the Welsh setting. Alfred Newman's score, also nominated, is lovely. All the ingredients add up to high point in cinema pleasure.

      This disc of How Green Was My Valley is very good looking. The immaculate compositions have been transferred from a print in excellent condition resulting in equally beautiful laser images. The sound is very clean and involving, with dialogue clear and easy to understand.  It was an enormous pleasure to share this film with my six year old daughter who seemed to enjoy it  immensely.
