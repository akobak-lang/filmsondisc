In the Bedroom/ A, A-
DVD Review

In the Bedroom. I mistakenly had thought the title referred to the relationship between a husband and wife, which I suppose it does, but it is also a phrase relating to lobster behavior when more than one is caught in a trap. In the Bedroom is a beautifully crafted film with superb acting across the board. Sometimes when a film receives such high praise it’s a let-down. In the Bedroom was anything but that. Todd Field, the writer director did a wonderful job as a storyteller with an economy of visual flourish. The characters life and breath and are developed even in their silences. Lots of surprises in the film. Not to be missed.

. Chief amongst them is In the Bedroom, the highly praised Academy Award nominee of last year. It wasn’t praised enough. What an intelligent film. The script, the directing, the acting are all first-rate. What makes the movie so remarkable is its rich multi-layered texture. It’s about relationships, fathers and sons, mothers and sons, husbands and wives; about friendships and facing life. The New England vistas of the Maine villages are dead on without hammering away at cloying charm. I look forward to seeing this one again, even soon.
