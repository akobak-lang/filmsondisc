Inspector Gadget/C+,A-

Disney/1997/125m/ANA 2.35

        The credit sequence of Inspector Gadget is delightful. There's lots of energy packed into the dynamic animation. The Disney World Main Street USA sequence that opens the film is suggests that this effects laden film might be a happy surprise. In fact, until the gadgets, characters and plot run amok, Inspector Gadget has some very nice moments.
