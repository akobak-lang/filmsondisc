Instinct
Disney/1999

Instinct

Anthony Hopkins is a compelling actor. There is ferocity about the way he approaches some roles and his Dr. Ethan Powell is one of those. So if you want to watch Hopkins fume and bare his teeth, Instinct may be your cup of stimulant. Trouble is, there’s not much of a story and the characters are barely sympathetic, if that. Personally, I identified more with the Gorillas than any of the human characters.

Instinct is one of those extinct psychological dramas that try ever so hard to make digging at the mind exciting.

I could never believe the physicality of Powell. The jungle scene supply little interest, in fact, they simply divert from the psychological drama and battle of the wits.
