Iron Giant/A,A

Warner/1999/87m/ANA 2.35

       How does a film as wonderful as The Iron Giant disappear from theaters with a puny box office. I mean, this animated delight was perfectly geared to modern audiences, cross-over audiences, kids and adults. What happened? Did someone drop the marketing ball. All the ruminations in the world won't change anything, but do make sure to catch this treat on DVD, because it's a beautiful movie beautifully delivered in an anamorphic widescreen transfer.
