Killer,The (SE)/B+,B
Winstar/1989/110/WS 1.85

     Everybody loves a killer when he's played by Chow Yun-fat, especially given the incredibly sympathetic portrait by John Woo in his definitive action flick, The Killer. Woo, who wrote and directed the sentimental brutal non-stop action movie, blends a terrific variety of ingredients to make The Killer a movie gourmet's delight. Stylistically borrowing from past masters of action like Sam Peckinpah or grand cinematic stylists like Martin Scorcese, Woo's The Killer 


    What sets The Killer above other Woo action cinema assaults is the strong character portraits developed within the strong script.
