King Solomon's Mines
MGM/1937/80/FS 1.33/BW

     It's old, it's creaky, but damned if it isn't fun. The classic H. Rider Haggard yarn about the hunt for the legendary diamonds of King Solomon plays with amble sense of humor in this British made version.
     I can't say that I'd expect to see Sir Cedric Hardwicke playing the hero in an adventure film, but that just what his Alan Quatermain is. Playing alongside Hardwicke with enough charm to tame a jungle is pretty Anna Lee.
