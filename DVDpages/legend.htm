Legend Director's Cut (SE)/ B-,B+
Universal/

    A pretty dark and frightening fairy tale. Scott creates an ambiance of overpowering evil.

When the group of saviors following the unicorn and the princess enters the dark castle the pacing slips a bit. It's most lively when the Dark Lord takes center stage with his vulgar seduction of the princess. The beauty of the production dissipates to a degree.

Greta make-up on the Dark Prince, though some of the other hideous creature creations don't work to the same effect.

©

    Legend has been given a beautifully atmospheric transfer by Universal. Depth of detail is outstanding. The many dark foreboding scenes have fine shadow balance revealing enough of the detail while the sunlit forest is bath in a bucolic light. The cinematic transfer is smooth and consistent. The dynamic range is outstanding with bright lights illuminating dramatically in the darker passages. I love the light reflected off the bubbles magically floating in the dark and cold forest night. The DTS soundtrack for Jerry Goldsmith's wonderful score is delivered with an open and wide range.
