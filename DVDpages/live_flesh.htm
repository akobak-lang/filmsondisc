Live Flesh/ B+,A
MGM/1997/101/ANA  2.35

Live Flesh from Almodovar. Man, can that guy make the potboilers sizzle. His use of color is so dynamic and exciting. Almodovar weepers have their own lively style. In Live Flesh the writer/director makes it all look like a candy colored film noir. Is that possible? In this case I think so.
