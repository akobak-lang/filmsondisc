Lord of the Rings: Fellowship of the Ring (SE) / B, B+
New Line/2001/178/ANA 2.35

 

©

Watched LOTR last night and I was very impressed by the transfer. It appears to be true to the material, though some of the CGI is quite flat and looks more like static matte paintings, but that's not the transfer. A few scenes are not resolved to the max, but again, that may well be the source material. Color saturation is excellent. It's a very bright transfer and there's no edginess. Reference? A tad under reference I would say. Dolby Digital EX 5:1 surround is too loud. Dialogue is clear and has a good force of power.
