Lost Son/B,B
DVD Review

. I can recommend a pretty neat little neo noir to you, The Lost Son, starring Daniel Auteuil. It's directed by Chris Menges. Auteuil is an excellent choice to play the somewhat sleazy private detective. At first glance, he's no more than a scam artist, so what follows is a surprise. Still, I don't think the characterization is consistent. This is one detective that probably got hit over the head a few too many times. His style is sloppy and incriminating to his friends. I don't think he would have lasted so long if his procedural talents were always in such disarray. Yet, even with the inconsistencies of character, some dubious plot machinations and some obvious conclusions, Auteuil survives with reputation unscathed.
