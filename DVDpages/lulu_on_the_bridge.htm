Lulu on the Bridge

Writer Paul Auster seeks out the unusual in his work. The Music of Chance was a brilliant small gemstone, cut perfectly to film by director Philip Haas.  Auster’s spare writing lends itself to film, so it is no surprise that Auster is now a hyphenate, writer/director. Auster work brings a metaphysical aspect to plain everyday reality. In Lulu on the Bridge, Auster goes further than ever before by leaving the realm of his naturalistic settings into a world set in motion by mysticism
