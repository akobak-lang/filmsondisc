Made (SE)/ C+, B
DVD Review

    Set two bozos loose on the world and you get Made. The humor resides in the characters. Plot concerns are thrown in to give the characters a dance floor. Some of the riffing is funny, but the skimpy, even dumb,  plot plays like a ball and chains around the characters' feet.
     

 

 

Tea or two. ©Artisan

    Some night scenes are surprisingly grainy, perhaps owing to low light photography, though it seems unlikely. Made is mostly sharp. There's some edge transition peaking, but it's mild. I encountered one digital drop-out.
   Favreau and Vaughn are chummy and comfortable on the audio commentary. It's not very technically oriented. You might have figured it would have been funnier, but the laughs are sparse.
