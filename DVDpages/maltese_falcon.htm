Maltese Falcon, The(SE)A,B+

Warner/1941/100m/FS 1.33 BW

    After watching the one hundred perfect minutes of The Maltese Falcon with a smile on my face, I thought I might have had lock-jaw. The polished gem of Dashiell Hammett's pulp detective novel never fails to delight. Every character, from detective Sam Spade to gunsel Wilmer Cook is a precious stone.
