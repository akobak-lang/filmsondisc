Man on the Moon/B+,A-
Universal/1999/119m/ABA 2.35

      Director Milos Forman is a gifted director. Who would have ever thought he could have made such a compelling movie about Hustler's confrontational publisher Larry Flynt, and once again, Forman defies the nature of the biopic by tackling a confrontational character's life, comedian Andy Kaufman.
