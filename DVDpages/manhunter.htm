Manhunter (SE)/A-,A-
Anchor Bay/

Michael Mann’s meticulous film constructions are sweet music to my ears. I marvel at the anal depictions, the rare attention to detail and the patience of the director. Manhunter could have been a much flashier film, but Mann explores the psychological ramifications on the FBI hunter Graham. It’s an interesting choice. The book spent almost half the time on the villain Francis Dollerhyde, which was great. Harris’s best I think. But the procedural material in Manhunter and Graham’s crime scene investigations are brilliant. The matter of Brian Cox versus Anthony Hopkins as Hannibal Lektor is an interesting debate. There’s something to be said for both portrayals. Cox is by far more chilling. This guy really would perform recreational surgery on you. Hopkins’ Leckor (sic) on the other hand is much more agreeable and fun to watch. Ultimately less chilling, he’s also much more of a pivitol character in Silence than in Manhunter
