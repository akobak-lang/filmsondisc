Mask/B+,B
DVD Review

-

 

The very touching story of Rocky Dennis, a teenager afflicted with a rare disease that left him with an increasing deformed face and cranial structure, the film surmounts the problems inherent in

    Cher is very interesting in the mother role of Rusty Dennis, and what a mother role indeed. Doing drugs every other scene, looking tough in leather, she’s often a contradiction. It’s a successful portrayal of a complex character. Wearing a huge amount of make-up is not an easy chore for an actor. Despite the added burden,    Stolz is likewise likable and solid wearing lots of make-up as Rocky.

     Mask is presented in a relatively grainy transfer. Grain is fine and moves consistently. Overall light output is very good, giving the widescreen plenty of head room for low light front projection systems.
