Molly/C,A-
MGM/1998/87m/ANA 1.85

     There's a sequence in Molly that tells the tale of this movie. Accompanying her brother to work and freshly out of her institutional home, Molly delights in passing the attractive fountain at where Buck works. You can see what's going through mind. After an embarrassing sequence in the office when she disrupts Buck's advertising pitch, Molly is sent with Buck's secretary to get pizza. Now, could you ever guess Molly winds up in the fountain, joyfully cavorting in the pulsing plumes of frothy water? On one hand, the spirit of the scene is delightful, on the other predictable and a cheap effect. Molly offers up a number of good, energetic moments, but the overall script construction is by the numbers and unconvincing. It's a patchwork movie that jumps about hyper kinetically, surviving only, at times, on the good will of Elisabeth Shue's screen personality. 

Molly cools down. ©MGM
