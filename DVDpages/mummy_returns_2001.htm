Mummy Returns 2001 (SE)/C+,A
Universal/2001/

 

The Mummy Returns. It was fun a lot of the time and the special effects were mostly dazzling, however, it did not go any place different than the first 1999 Mummy and executed the plots lines with somewhat less snap. There are undoubtedly more special effects in the sequel.
