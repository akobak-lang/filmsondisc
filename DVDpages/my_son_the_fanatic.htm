My Son the Fanatic/B+,B+
Miramax/1998/87m/ANA 1.77

     Love crops up in the most unexpected places and doesn't always make sense on the surface. We see countless movies with younger women falling in love with older men and we wonder why. My Son the Fanatic ups the ante a notch. The older man is an Pakastani immigrant cab driver, into late middle age, short, stout, not an obvious candidate for a love affair. The younger women is a prostitute, Now we know there is a cinematic symbiosis between drivers and prostitutes. Some movies depict these drivers as Knights in dark armor protecting ladies of limited virtue.
