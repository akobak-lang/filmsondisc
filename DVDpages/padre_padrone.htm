Padre Padrone/A-,D
DVD Review

Padre Padrone

The Taviani Brothers film Padre Padrone based on the book by Gavino Ledda is consistently powerful in its presentation of the life of peasants of Sardinia. Ledda, brought up as a shepherd boy, a tool of his father's will, escaped his origins intellectually, but as he tells his story, the power of the countryside and the family overwhelm everything else.

The brutality of spirit that dominates much of the film

The primitive score has elements of innocence and horror

This is one of the worst DVDs I recall watching from star to finish. There is so much grain in the image, it appears that it came from inferior source material. The transfer also seems slip shod as it appears there is dirt lodged on the transfer machinery in a great many scenes; just an added bit of frustration. The power of the filmed images are muted and mangled by the poor quality of this DVD. Shame, shame on someone! The yellow English subtitles are included on the image. They are easy to read and well sized.
