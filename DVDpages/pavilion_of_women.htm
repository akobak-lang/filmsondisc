Pavilion of Women/ B-,B
Universal/2001/116//ANA 1.85

    Blatantly old-fashioned melodrama filmed in a lush style, Pavilion of Women could easily fall under much of the stiff posturing were it not for the formal and stiff setting of rigidly structured China. 

 

 

Father Andre gets seduced by China. ©Universal

    While the colors are saturated with wonderful richness, overall sharpness leaves a little to be desired. Some scenes fall a little flat. Still, the cinematography provides many beautiful compositions that show off DVD very well.
