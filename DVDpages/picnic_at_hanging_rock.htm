Picnic at Hanging Rock/B+,B+
 

     Picnic at Hanging Rock is hypnotic in setting up and laying out the mystery of the disappearance of several girls on a school outing. Screened through the vision of director Peter Weir, the mystery takes on other-worldly qualities. It's almost a ghost story, yet it is simply a recounting of the events surrounding the disappearance.
