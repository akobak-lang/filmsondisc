Pledge, The/B+,B+

     A icy wind blows through The Pledge thoroughly permeating its fabric. This hard-edged psychological drama isn't always easy to watch in its uncompromising viewpoint. Nevertheless, the material, while not perfectly presented, is fascinating from beginning to end.

     This is a great performance from Jack Nicholson. Rather unadorned by typical Nicholson mannerisms, Jerry Black
