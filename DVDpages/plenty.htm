Plenty/C+,B
 

A dramatic opening offers great promise of fascinating characters, excitement and romance in Nazi occupied France.

With the gradual decline of its main character, Plenty too descends an unending spiral into boredom.  The actors are outstanding and some of the scenes come find an emotional core, but this is a cold screenplay, making it difficult to enjoy any of the characters. Yes, you want to take sides with someone, but the warts are all too prominent.

There are a number of lighting schemes that define the progressive aspects of Plenty. The DVD masters the cool images of England, contrasting them accurately with the heat of Morocco. Overall, the disc is quite sharp with a few slightly soft sequences. Color is stable and flesh tones are accurate.
