Pola X
DVD Review

    A pervasive 19th century air floats through Pola X. The overwrought  melodramatic machinations are stylistically from another time.  I did not know it was based on Herman Melville's Pierre of the Ambiguities. That doesn't mean the fusion of the modern setting and the mysterious anachronistic emotional core of Pola X is any more satisfying. At it's best, Pola X is a brave film that strives to achieve classic heights and at its worst, it's confusing, frustrating, and lack of logical motivation mires it a twisted mass of fuzzy celluloid.
