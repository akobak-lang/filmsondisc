      The play book is familiar. The de rigor segments of The Replacements are predictable and don't go the extra yard. The description of the players for the replacement team, for instance, and then the try-outs for the cheer leaders, is by the numbers film-making that yields minimal laughs only.

Advice to the quarterback. ©Warner

     Gene Hackman is such a good actor, it's a shame to watch him in a throwaway part like Jimmy McGinty. It's not thta Hackman is not charming. His signature laugh still captures the screen turf, but his character is shallow in concept and shallow in execution. Keanu Reeves is actually quite well cast as the Quarterback who never made it.
