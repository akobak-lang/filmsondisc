Semi-Tough/B,C+
DVD Review

Last night I watched Semi-Tough, the humorous football buddy movie starring Burt Reynolds in top form. Kris Kristofferson is a bit too charming for my taste. There’s too much evidence of his drool slobbering over Jill Clayburgh. Overall, the movie is semi-alright. Directed by Michie Ritchie in likeable fashion, there’s no bite to the humor, unlike the excellent Ted Kotcheff directed North Dallas Forty starring Nick Nolte.  The transfer, non-anamorphic, is semi-okay. It’s too soft at times, though the color is quite good. There’s little need for shadow detail as the production is consistently bright and never subtle. It depends on the comic chemistry of three principals to succeed.
