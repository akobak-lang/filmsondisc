Siege, The /B,A-

Fox/1973/143m/WS 1.85

   The most far-fetched elements of Ed Zwick's The Siege are a lot closer to reality today than when the film was made several years ago.  Director Ed Zwick explores all terrorism, fanatics, abuse of power, and a challenge to the American way in the course of less than two hours in the often exciting but over-baked new film.  The acknowledgement that we are all so vulnerable, that the more we have to lose the more exposed we are a harsh reality, but one that we should always remember. 

Running with spirals. ©Columbia
      The Siege DVD is an exemplary example of extracting the most from the package you choose to deliver. The widescreen DVD is extremely sharp and detailed even without the additional benefit of anamorphic treatment. There were no NTSC apparent artifacts. Fox has often been guilty of excessive enhancement, but somehow they have managed to make this DVD look so sharp without compromise. All the colors are perfectly natural. There’s a nice variation to skin tones. Contrast is outstanding is all lighting situations. There is some significant hesitation on the layer change, so much so in fact that I had to swing back to make sure I didn’t miss something.
