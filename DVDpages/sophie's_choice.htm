Sophie’s Choice
DVD Review

 

Alan Pakula’s admirable production of the devastating William Styron novel is brought to the screen with all of the director’s good taste. Though on analysis the film is quite claustrophobic, Pakula creates the feeling of a huge canvas, spanning Sophie’s present in the 1950s to glimpses of the terrible reality of her removal to a concentration camp. 

Meryl Streep gives one of the most amazing performances of her career and credit Pakula for making her look as good as possible. There’s a moment when she screams but Pakula chooses to make it a silent scream. It is utterly shattering in its power. A great moment for the actress and an even greater moment for the director.b

The documentary accompanying the feature of this DVD achieves more than the typical effort made for special editions. Including talking heads of survivors of the death camp brings home the reality of the holocaust all the more poignantly.
