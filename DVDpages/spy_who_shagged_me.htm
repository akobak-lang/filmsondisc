Austin Powers: The Spy Who Shagged Me(SE)/C+, B+

New Line/19731999/143m/WS 1.85

      The Spy Who Shagged Me and ripped my heart out because he was all too gross; that’s my take on the new Austin Powers romp. Austin is a little less charming this time out, Doctor Evil is handicapped with the addition of an odious little clone

     I like the concept of Robert Wagner as a contemporary Number Two and Rob Lowe playing the younger version as Austin zooms off into the past to recover his mojo. It made for some nice bit, especially as Frobischer has not changed a single hair on her head, only her preference for men.

     One look at the collection of new characters is enough warning that Meyers and company have veered too far to the disgusting side. The aforementioned Mini-Me, Austin's midget clone, is a rather annoying little pest. He'd just as soon take a bite out of your leg as take the Doctor Evil pinky pose. Fat Bastard, played with pure disgusting gusto by Meyers, is in one word, disgusting. Does that make him funny? Not in my book. Fat Bastard should have spent six months at a body reduction clinic before being exposed to vi

Rehashing some of the first Austin Powers movie is not unreasonable. I almost wish Meyers did more of it. The delicious blocked privates bit is milked for more lauighs than the rest of the film put together in the credit sequence and Meyers finds more ways to skin a weener than I can count. Aside from those bits, I can't get too excited about machine gun robotic breasts, even if they are Elizabeth Hurley's, and who came up with the awful wardrobe Heather Graham is forced to wear in Meyers madcap vision?

Meyers can do Austin blindfolded. With the various lens' he is forced to wear in character, I wouldn't be surprised if he is doing the movie blindfolded half the time. He's funny, but never consistent. Meyers will take a shot at anything and if it fails, usually move on to something entertaining. Too often in The Spy Who Shagged Me he fails to move on, stuck in time with his chosen army of unfunny new comic characters.
