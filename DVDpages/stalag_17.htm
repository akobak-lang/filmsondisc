Stalag 17/A-

Paramount/1953/120m/BW/FS 1.33

    Stalag 17 is Billy Wilder's adaptation of a stage play, but Wilder's film never feels stage bound. Maybe it's the lively nature of the characters and the dominent portrayal of William Holden as Sefton, the cynical POW with a way to get his way.

Sefton is accused again. ©Paramount
