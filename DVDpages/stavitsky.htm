Stavitsky
DVD Review

   the high finance concepts are quite interesting. He's a character who understands that the higher his financial profile, the more difficult it will be for his creditors to bring him down. Sound familiar?

©

   I rather like Charles Boyer as the Baron. He is the epitome of cool, a man apparently quite contented with the realization of his own shortcomings.

Belmondo is an enthusiastic Stavitsky.

It's a very soft transfer. It looks blown out. Black level is too high. Yellow English subtitles are very sharp and easy to read.
