Straight Story/B+,B+
DVD Review

Straight Story. It's a finely crafted, intimate portrait of a man doing what he must do, reviewing his life on a cross state trip to visit his estranged brother. Richard Farnsworth is a special actor. His passive yet luminous face conveys reams of material. David Lynch decided there should be no chapter stops on the DVD because films should be viewed continuously. Well, I for one discovered Lynch's posturing after watching the movie straight through without an interruption, but when I wanted to look at a couple of scenes again, no chapters. Thanks for the pretension David.
