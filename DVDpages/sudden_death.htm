Sudden Death/B
DVD Review

Sudden Death

B, A-

US/1995/Color/Widescreen 2.35:1/Stereo Surround/111 minutes/Directed by Peter Hyams/Starring Jean-Claude Van Damme, Powers Boothe/MCA-Universal/46 Chaps/CLV/$34.98

 

The claustrophobic setting for Sudden Death is ideal thriller material and director Peter Hyams makes excellent use of the Pittsburgh Hockey Arena for lots of suspense mingled with the roar of the crowd. Okay, Sudden Death does not break any new ground, but the non-stop pacing leaves little time to question the faulty logic of the script equation and it is only on review after completion of the film that some of the screenplay silliness fails scrutiny.

Jean-Claude Van Damme plays a former heroic fireman now working as a  stadium fire marshal who is forced to reinvent himself when his children become ensnared by a terrorist plot to hold the arena for ransom. The action races through the dark passages of the bowels of the stadium and soars to the roof and rafters. The camera takes every opportunity for nighttime aerial views of the arena. Hyams’ crisp editing gives the film an added edge and acting as his own director of photography he gets he delivers a slick and pleasing production.

Jean-Claude Van Damme  once again teams with Hyams after the very good Timecop and displays all the skills necessary to make hero Darren McCord a believable character. Van Damme is an athletic, straight ahead guy who does whatever he has to do to save his family and foil the villains. The terrorists, predictably, are less convincing. Powers Boothe extracts the maximum from his role as the mastermind of the impossible plot, but the script provides too many pithy moments for his to display his brand of menace.

Sudden Death has been turned into an aggressive laser disc. Night scenes are delivered with a high gloss making pitch perfect background for contrasting explosions. There’s nothing subtle about this laser disc. The sound track pounds with deep bass. There’s plenty of surround pazazz for action fans. A first-rate laser disc.
