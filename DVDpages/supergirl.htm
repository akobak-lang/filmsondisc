Supergirl/C,B
Anchor Bay/1984/124/ANA 2.35

     Camp or crap? That is the question. Or somewhere in between? You have to believe the makers of Supergirl were going for the former, but there seems to be too much Kryptonite hanging around the screenplay and production decisions to produce any real fun. 

     Whatever is the story behind the sudden appearance of Supergirl's costume and her magical change into school girl's clothing. She sure discovers her powers post haste.
    Anchor Bay has made a real big effort to make Supergirl a super DVD, but the film elements leave something to be desired. Blue screen work ain't too pretty, though Slater's costume fits well and the colors and curves stand out nicely. The transfer is usually sharp enough to show off the glowing pink make-up adding to Slater's innocent look.
