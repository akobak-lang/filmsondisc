Suzhou River/B+,C+
DVD Review

Suzhou River is a fascinating Chinese neo noir. It’s perhaps the most heavily narrated film aside from documentaries that my memory can recall. Director Le You films almost everything from a first-person perspective. You become one with the camera and the narrator. This is certainly a tale about obsession, but it’s a very romantic, off-beat love story as well. Set in modern-day Shanghai, a videographer living along the Suzhou River is called upon to video a mermaid show at a local night club. He’s fascinated by the woman slithering in costume in a water tank. But, the central focus of the film is not the videographer. Just when you think you have this film figured out, it turns in a parallel direction dissecting another tale of love and obsession. Everything eventually ties together with captivating passion. The DVD is not bad. It’s not an anamorphic job, but it’s reasonably sharp and artifacts are kept to a minimum. Contrast range could have been somewhat more tightly controlled. But the transfer is probably true to the artistic vision and the original source material.
