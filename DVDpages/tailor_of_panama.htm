Tailor of Panama/A-,A
DVD Review

I watched a terrific flick in a absolutely gorgeous transfer. John Boorman’s Tailor of Panama is a wonderfully sly spy movie. The humor is never laugh out loud, but the smile on my face was only interrupted on occasion by some of the violence images that shaped the characters. Andy Oxnard, the name seems conjured up from a pig-Latin fest. Pierce Brosnan is marvelously obnoxious as the slimy spy looking for a rich way out of the game. Geoffrey Rush is wonderful as Harry Pendel, the slightly smarmy would-be Saville Row tailor who has tried to cloth his past behind a flawless accent and impeccably tailored suits. The Panama streets are an explosion of color: incredibly saturated reds, bright strong yellows, green to die for and a bright punchy picture that emphasizes Boorman’s wry sense of humor.
