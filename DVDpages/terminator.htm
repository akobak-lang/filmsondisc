Terminator (SE) /B+,B
MGM/1984/

     A ferociously driven Scifi flick. With straight forward action with single minded determination, writer/director James Cameron produces a paradigm of power-packed visual storytelling. Exposition is limited to just enough to know what's going  on.

 

 

©

    A, good, solid transfer, likely limited by the source material. It's pretty sharp, but not as detailed      Contrast range is fine. Lots of dark scenes, again, could have had more punch.
   Cameron's comments over the deleted scenes are excellent. He articulates his decisions with insight. You learn a lot about the film focus by seeing what was trimmed or deleted.
