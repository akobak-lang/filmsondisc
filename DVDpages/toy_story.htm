Toy Story/A,A
Disney/1995/

     The animation may be scrumptious, but it's only shown to its best advantage because of the creative minds involved in making this wonderful animated film for all ages.

©

 

    The scope of the extras on the individual DVDs is fantastic. I especially liked the "Multi-Language Reel" dubbed into thirty languages. There's audio commentary, "Toy Story Treats" consisting of short segments not in the film, the award winning short Tin Toy and The making of Toy Story. If you want the complete picture, buy the box set which includes a third DVD that presents everything you ever wanted to know about Toy Story and its sequel.
     Transferred in the digital domain to DVD, Toy Story is breathtakingly spectacular. I cannot imagine DVD looking better than this.
