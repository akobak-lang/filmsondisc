DVD Review

Triump

of the Spirit. It stars Willem Dafoe, Robert Loggia and Edward James

Olmos. It is a fantastic film directed by Robert Young and was filmed at

Auschwitz, I believe. The true conentration camp story of a Jewish Greek

boxer and his family. The performances are excrutiatingly real. Olmos's

gypsy is mesmerizing.

It not something you want to watch, yet you can't take your eyes from the screen. Director Young is fairly reserved in presenting the material. You can only imagine how much worse it was and it's therefore all the more powerful. Even the Nazis are depicted as complex individuals.

Bright transfer with good light output and good contrast. Shadow detail is revealing. Lighting in the dark scenes is effective. Good range of fleshtones.  Detail is pretty good with a couple of scenes less sharp than they might have been.
