Under the Sand (SE)/ A, B+
Fox Lorber/2000/95/ANA 1.85

      Under the Sand moves with swift emotional force that belies the raw material. The intimate portrait scrutinizes the internal pain and external élan of Marie Drillon with  exquisite understanding.

Jean Drillon is delivered with natural power by Bruno Cremer.

 

Marie looks out at the ocean. ©Fox Lorber

    This is perhaps the video transfer released on DVD by Fox Lorber. Excellent color saturation and a very film-like  English subtitles are easy to read  The Dolby Digital 5:1 surround encoding is up at least 10dB. Otherwise, it is clean with a measure of surround information delivered accurately. The music is appropriately lush and moody.  Special edition includes an introspective look at the project from Charlotte Rampling. A audio commentary delivered in French by Francois Ozon and  co-writer Emmanuelle Bernheim includes an English subtitle track. Excellent give and take between the participants revealing the ambiguities and clarity of the plot.
