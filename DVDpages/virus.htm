Virus/C,C+

Universal/1998/100m/ANA 2.35

Virus/C,C+ 

When the title of a movie is as misleading as Virus, it could be taken as a clue that the filmmakers were in rough plot seas. Virus takes place on the high seas during a nasty typhoon, but those are the easiest moments for the crew and actors of this derivative movie since the rumple and crash of the water drowns out most everything else. Once you get into plot, Virus gets into trouble.

When the storm hits the fan.  ©Universal
I encountered a Macrovision violation message when trying to access the chapter list on the DVD menu. This is a first for me. While an inconvenience, I was able to scroll through the chapters with the next chapter function as normal.
