Wall Street/B+,B
Fox/1987/126/ANA 1.85

     The yuppie broker bible comes to DVD with resonance in tact for year 2000. Gordon Gekko, played with sheer delight by Michael Douglas,

©

    Oliver Stone is a brilliant observer of life, a man of strong opinions and often lucid vision. Listening to Stone's commentary on Wall Street is like getting a surprise stock dividend declared for one of your investments. Stone's memories of making Wall Street are filled with keen details about artistic intent and technical aspects of filmmaking. A solid documentary by Charles Kiselyak made for the DVD includes talking heads of Stone and stars intercut with scenes from the film.

    Overall, the DVD is a good representation of what Stone and Richardson wanted captured on film. Some of the interiors are a mite dark and grungy, but there is an aspect to the bastion of Gordon Gekko that needs an shady emphasis. Images are resolved with consistent detail level and there is only a touch of edge enhancement discernible in a few scenes. Stewart Copeland's score get a great Dolby 5:1 mix. Memorable dialogue is served clearly.
