Way of the Gun
DVD Review

    Watching Way of the Gun I was reminded of Robert Rodriguez's fresh and thoroughly enjoyable El Mariachi, that director's debut film of several years ago. Way of the Gun, Usual Suspects writer Christopher McQuarrie's first shot behind the camera displays some of the visual style of that other debut film, but it lacks character charm and the stylistic execution seems to serve only the style, not the film.
