Will Penny/A, B+
Paramount

    Many consider Will Penny o be Charlton Heston's finest acting. I'd have to concur. It's a beautifully unadorned performance, unafraid, warts prominently protruding. Joan Hackett is terrific with Hackman. She can look plain and beautiful from alternate angles. She's a strong presence and captures the feeling of a real pioneer woman. Donald Pleasance has a field day playing Preacher Quint, a father, not unlike an Ike Clanton in My Darling Clementine. But it's Heston's film.

    Terrific script from debut director Tom Gries. He paints the pictures of the west with grand authenticity. Gries son does some decent work as young Jon Gries has good chemistry with Heston as Horace Allen. Bruce Dern gets to exercise his well-honed nasty boy look as Rafe Quint.
