AFI 100 Editor's Choice

Best ten lists are next impossible. Selecting any list is a daunting challenge and even a list of 100 out of 400 has more than its share of difficult choices. Many great films have been left off the list of 400, such as John Ford's Drums Along the Mohawk, Robert Rossen's Body and Soul, Mark Robson's Champion and the fabulous Alexander MacKendrick film The Sweet Smell of Success. The list of omissions is likely enormous, but AFI's parameters are broad and leave room for lots of selection latitude and, sadly, exclusion. My list of titles appear in red.
