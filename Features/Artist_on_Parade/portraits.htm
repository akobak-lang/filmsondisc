Portraits

 

Many biographical films have been made about great painters. Charles Laughton portrays Rembrandt as a condescending genius, trapped by his own passion for the paint. Kirk Douglas is a tortured Vincent Van Gogh in director Vincent Minelli's Lust for Life. The only Rembrandt painting truly focused upon in that film is The Night Watch. The painting is the pivotal element of the plot as Rembradt's fortunes turn on that one painting. Lust for Life, on the other hand, offers up a colorful catalogue of Van Gogh's striking compositons, juxtaposing scenes of the painter's life with those images captured by his conciousness and transposed onto spectacular canvases.

 

There is another group of films that focus on a single painting, the camera examing its every aspect. The painting becomes part of the plot and indeed acts as character or catalyst or both. Laura is the perfect example. We are introduced to the title character through her portrait. Detective......falls in love with the imafge of Laura, even though he believes her dead. The portrait sets in motion the narrative, functioning in place of flesh and blood Gene Tierney until she is finally introduced.

 

The greatest of all portrait films is The Picture of Dorian Grey.

 

Ever wonder what happed to the paintings created for those memorable films. Dorian Gray's portrait was paintied by Edwin Albrecht.

 

Portrait of Jenny is another film that uses the painting as a starting point to draw its audience into a mystery.

 

In The Light That Failed, Roanld Coleman gives another elegant screen performance as painter Dick Helgar, the hero of Rudyard Kipoling's first novel. The film is very successful in its interpretation by director Willian Wekllman. This picture too pivots on one painting, a portrait of  a Londsown Prostitute. The painting is destroys in a fury by the model, Ida Lupino in a searing screen performance.

 

A happenstance glance by Edward G. Robinson at a portrait of model Joan Bennett in a gallery sets of tale vituperous intrigue in the Fritz Lang thriller, Woman in the Window.
