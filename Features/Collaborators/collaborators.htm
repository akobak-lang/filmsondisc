The Collaborators

      The old adage used to be "Don't break up a winner." That was referring to baseball, but moviemakers have taken heed to the wisdom over the years.
     Did you know that beginning with Drunken Angel in 1948, Akira Kurosawa directed Toshiro Mifune in ten movies. It was no coincidence that Kurosawa kept going back to Mifune as his star. Time after time Mifune came up to bat for Kurosawa and delivered.
