Infinifilm:
Redefining the Special Edition

    Is Infinifilm just a clever public relations campaign to brand New Line's special editions? Judging from the initial Infinifilm DVD release Thirteen Days, this new breed of special edition stretches the limits of the DVD medium to provide a huge amount of historical supplemental material to compliment the feature film. Part of the Infinifilm hype is that these special editions will be easier for Joe Public to understand and fully utilize.
     "The basic premise of Infinifilm is special features that take you beyond the movie in a consistent manner and format." explains Mike Mulvihill, New Line Home Entertainment's Vice President of Content Development.
