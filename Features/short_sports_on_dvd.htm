      Tiger Woods has just done what no golfer since Ben Hogan has accomplished, winning three of the four major golf championships in one year. There's even a TV biopic being made about Tiger Woods. but what about Ben Hogan.
     Hogan was the legendary golfer who came back from a severe auto accident to capture the hearts of golf and sports fans everywhere. They made a movie about Hogan's life too called Follow the Sun, starring Glenn Ford and the gutsy golfer with Anne Baxter providing loyal support as his wife.

     Follow the Sun is a pretty good sports biopic that never even made is to laser disc, much less DVD. In fact, there's a dearth of sports movies on DVD.
