Two Sides of the Same Coin
AAA

Social concience and heroine are two sides of the Kurosawa coin. It may be obvious when Kurosawa is working in the millieu of social concious films. Whether dealing with government buracracy or the abject poverty of post World War II Japan, Kuroswa's point of view is worn on his sleeve like a military medal. But that same element invades the lansape and infues it in his jingadeki films as well.
