BLUE KITE/B+, B

CHINA/1993/YELLOW SUBTITLES/COLOR/WIDESCREEN 1.77:1/139 MINUTES/DIRECTED BY TIAN ZHUANGZHUANG/STARRING LU LIPING, PU QUANXIN/KINO/31 CHAPS/CLV/$49.99

 

While other films from China recently have depicted the full political sweep of China from the breakdown of the Manchu seat to the vicissitudes of the Cultural Revolution, The Blue Kite concentrates its focus on the years of Mao’s "Great Leap Forward" through the wild upheaval of the Red Guards. Again, a Chinese filmmaker weaves a story about survival through uncertainty. The plight of the Chinese people as depicted in The Blue Kite is that they do not know how they must act to survive. It is a world ruled by the capricious winds of unpredictable change.

The Blue Kite is seen through the eyes of a child looking back at China’s political upheaval. It is the story of how his family attempts to survive and is a hard hitting ice cold splash of reality dealt up by its director Tian Zhuangzhuang. Tian is unrelenting in his portrayal of the cold harshness of these times. Part of the power of The Blue Kite is that it deals with very ordinary people trying to live up to the ideals of their country. It’s a tall order. Caught between the proverbial rock and hard place, there seems to be no way out but to turn to rock yourself. But, in the end, it is one’s humanity that provides a hope for salvation.

The actors are very skillful in naturalistic performances. Lu Liping builds an armor of pathos around her portrayal of the mother. The various children who play the narrator Tietou are very effective under the guidance of this fine director. The photography of The Blue Kite has a similar naturalistic feel to it. The music is highly effective.

The Blue Kite is presented in clean laser images. Contrasts have a moderate dynamic range, but grain is minimal. The pressing had a number of horizontal dropouts, but not enough to disturb the delicate compositions. The sound is very open and the strands of music are enveloping. The yellow subtitles are presented on the image and are easy to read.
