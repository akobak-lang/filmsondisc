Ransom/B, A

US/1996/Color/Widescreen 1.85:1/Stereo Surround,DD/139 minutes/Directed by Ron Howard/Starring Mel Gibson, Gary Sinese/Touchstone/22 Chaps/CLV/THX/$39.99

Ransom is a beautifully produced film that seems like it should play better than it does. Despite an excellent cast and a script plotted with enough tense moments for two films, Ransom fails to meet its own demands.

High profile airline owner Tom Mullen’s son is kidnapped right under the nose of his parents. The team of kidnappers keeps the young boy bound and blindfolded in a hideout until the ransom demands are met. The only problem is that there are too many wild cards in the deck. mastermind

I can’t quite put my finger on the problem with Ransom. Yes, it’s overlong and the added eighteen minutes in the laser release seems only to make it longer rather than motivate the characters and action better. The setting for the snatch, in the midst of a Central Park Science Fair Awards ceremony is stretches reality. The actors are all quite good, yet there is an emotional barrier that prevents total audience involvement. Maybe the pacing of Ransom saps some of the inherent story strength.

Mel Gibson is fine as Tom Mullen, yet he fails to capture the sympathy of the audience. Gary Sinese is a spooky kidnapper. The usually dependable Delroy Lindo lacks credibility as an FBI agent. Rene Russo lacks any real passion as Mullen’s wife. My hunch is that the script draws its characters with slightly crooked lines, failing to meet audience expectations. This could serve as a great attribute to a film, but it doesn’t work in Ransom. Still, Ransom is a good film, just slightly enigmatic in its lack of fire.

This is a beauty of a laser disc. You couldn’t ask for sharper images without NTSC artifacts. The many darks scenes at the kidnap hideout are transferred with extraordinary effect. Light and shadow are always in balance. Color is absolutely natural. Reds are intense with no bleeding. The James Horner score is powerful with tight base and excellent dynamic range.
