Movie Memory Project

     Do you remember your first exposure to the movies? Reach back in time and think about what brought you to love the movies. Was it the first time you got scared to death by the reality of the big screen?
     My first movie memory is not exactly of the movie, but of the experience of going to the movies. I am sure it wasn't the first time I had gone to the movies, but it lingers in my memory for a number of reasons. Partially because it was a family event of sorts. My family was int he city with another family to see a movie on the great white way. The Times Square area was a circus of lights, dancing neon, Mr. Peanut and a huge camel cigarette billboard blowing smoke rings around the movie fantasy world.
