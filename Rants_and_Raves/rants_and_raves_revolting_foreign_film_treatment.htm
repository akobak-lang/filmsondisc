Revolting Foreign Film Treatment

     Just wanted to get this off my chest a bit. The disgusting treatment of foreign films on DVD. Some of the shoddiest source materials are dug up by so-called champions of independent and foreign cinema. Take Kino International for instance: They put out lots of fine foreign DVD titles, but it seems like they treat the material and the consumer like yesterday's garbage. I just watched the fascinating Code Unknown directed by Michael Haneke. It looked like crapola. Their release of Bertrand Tanvernier's Captaine Conan was unwatchable. Kadosh, Life on a String, and were terrible. Isn't there some responsibility to put some effort to deliver a decent looking DVD. Winstar/Fox Lorber (Wellspring now) is another company that has consistently shortchanged the movies they release and the consumers who purchase them. On the other hand, majors like Sony and Disney has usually produced beautiful DVDs from their foreign films. Image ENtertainment too has made an attempt to get good source material and deliver good looking DVDs. Anchor Bay found fine elements for Paul Verhoeven's early films.
