HOT FLICKS

Savior
A devastating portrait of the war in the Balkans. Dennis Quaid makes an interesting mercenary.

Shakespeare in Love
Delightful entertainment. Catch Shakespeare in his underwear with his inspiration exposed. The anamorphic DVD looks divine. Shakespeare in Love is the inaugural ISF DVD Selection of the Month. Congratulations to Miramax and Disney.

October Sky
Soaring drama about four young men who rise above their coal mine origins. Great filmmaking from director Joe Johnston. Outstanding transfer. Not to be missed!
