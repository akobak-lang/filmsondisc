HOT FLICKS

The Adventures of Baron Munchhausen
Terry Gilliam's wild screen fantasy is one of the great movie visual feasts. How many chances will you ever get to see Uma Thurman on the half shell:)

The Vanishing
The original Dutch thriller directed by George Sluizer is a no compromise horrific vision. You may never feel the same about tight places again. Skip the flaccid Hollywood remake, ironically directed by Sluizer again.

Much Ado About Nothing
Kenneth Branagh does the Bard again with enormous success. One of the best Shakespeare adaptation ever. Wonderful cast, ebullient direction in an a gem of an anamorphic DVD.
