Fastballs in Japan

Newly arrived  on DVD, the delightful comedy Mr. Baseball may take liberties with the conventions of a foreigner playing baseball in Japan but you might find it is closer to reality than the comedy suggests. The accompanying  excerpts from an 1972 interview conducted in Kobe, Japan with an American ballplayer going through everything Jack Elliot endures might shed some additional light on the humor in Mr. Baseball.

Want to compare the home run swing of Jack Elliot to  former  Japanese Home Run King Daryl Spencer? Click on the baseball for Fastballs in Japan.
