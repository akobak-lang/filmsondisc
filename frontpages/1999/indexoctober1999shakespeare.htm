HOT FLICKS

Little Fugitive
An insightful film about childhood and a document to a world gone by. Check out the camera use thirty odd years before the advent of the Steadicam. 

October Sky
Soaring drama about four young men who rise above their coal mine origins. Great filmmaking from director Joe Johnston. Outstanding transfer. Not to be missed!
