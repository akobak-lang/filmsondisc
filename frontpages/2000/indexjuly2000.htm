The Return of an Epic

Awesome news for movie lovers. Winstar Cinema is releasing Akira Kurosawa's epic masterpiece Ran to theaters around the country beginning August 18. The release prints have been struck from pristine elements stored by producer Serge Silberstein in Paris. Ran is one of the most strikingly photographed films ever made. The magnificent battle scene compositions are filmed with the majesty of a great master. Don't miss an opportunity to see Ran in theaters in all it's original widescreen glory.

Click on the Ran poster to visit Winstar's site for a great profile of the movie including pictures and production history. You can also check out The Epic Images of Kurosawa for Stu Kobak's take on Ran and several other masterworks by the great Japanese director.
