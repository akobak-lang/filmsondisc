Haircuts for the Sake of Art

By Stu Kobak

     A faux pas at dinner tonight got me to thinking about Hollywood haircuts. Not the perfectly coiffed variety, but the abominations that actors and actresses have had to endure for the sake of their art.

     Sharon Stone, Eleanor Parker and other actresses have shaved their hair for their art.  The shape of their heads was fully exposed for everyone to see, but that's not so bad. When you are a beautiful woman
