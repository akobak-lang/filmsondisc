Insert Posters

Click on the thumbnail posters for larger images

Inserts measure 14 x 36. Their unique shape presented a challenge to the poster artists which often meant more interesting art than the one-sheet or half sheet. Inserts were most often released on a card stock. Like half sheets, inserts that have been folded can suffer deterioration in the creased areas.
     Posters for  films based on books like to feature a book image in the artwork like on the Mortal Storm poster. Calcutta, a great example of Paramount's finest poster art, emphasizes the exotic setting and mystery theme with a complex composition of images. Posters may usually feature stars, but in the case of the One, Two Three poster designed by Saul Bass, director Billy Wilder gets to sit atop the ladder for his comedy.
