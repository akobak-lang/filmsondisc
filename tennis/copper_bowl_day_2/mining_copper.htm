 Mining Copper

January, 2 2000

     It was a beautiful day at the Copper Bowl. Liz played her second round match at the Foothills High School. Twelve great courts in the middle of the desert. Pictured above with opponent Randi Schuler of Davis, California after her beautifully fought three set victory for Liz,  6-4, 4-6, 6-4. Sportsmanship was at its highest level; no line calls were questioned by either player despite the closeness of the match. Both girls hit hard and painted the lines. Liz was able to hit one more back in the end. Dad Fred Schuler was so nervous he could not remember how old his daughter was and missed it by almost a year. When we checked on the computer, we saw a different birth date and had a laugh the next day with the Schulers.

Dorothy and Fred exchange friendlies before the match.

 

     With Lizzie's match finishing at around 2 PM, we had a rather late lunch at a restaurant called Wildflowers. It was the second lunch we did there and it was pleasant again. Big salads and big smiles.

Liz with AJ Nelson at the magic show.

      Liz had a very light dinner before heading for the Tucson Mall player party sponsored by the Copper Bowl. Kids met up at the food court and were supposed to shop together. We think the tournament director has found a way to make money off every aspect of this event. The mall had a magic show and we hung around with the Nelsons from Ohio and watched a bit. Liz had fun trying on the youngest Nelson's hat. No shopping from the Kobaks, yassuh, yassuh.

The stringing room at El Conquistador

     There was one last stop before sneaking under the sheets for the night. Liz had a pair of racquets restrung at the El Conquistador. Pictured above with Liz and Dorothy are Domingo Jordan and the number one seed daughter Ale. Craig Bothman, a principal in Pro Circuit Stringing is at the stringing machine. The Pro Circuits guy deliver consistently every time. Each trip to the stringing room has been a pleasure with light banter accompanying the pulling of the strings.
