Destination Arizona

December 26, 2000

Liz and Dorothy are enjoying the sun in Tucson outside our condominium. We still need to bundle up since the morning's temperatures are under forty. The accommodations are good enough to keep the girls smiling since our bedrooms are far apart.


Stu is trying to keep up with Films on Disc during the tennis road trip. The laptop PC serves a dual purpose here, acting as a portable office and a lap warmer in the cool of the morning.

 

The winds at the El Conquistador Country Club were whipping fiercely Christmas day but Liz still had time to get her racquet back to rip a practice return.

Looks like Dorothy has  a big smile for the surrounding space and the photographer as well. We are staying at the Golf Villas at Oro Valley.
