 Quarters

December 29, 2000

     Another incredible day of tennis for Liz at the USTA Winter National 14s. In the round of 16 Liz's opponent was hard hitter Alexis Plukas of Massachusetts. Plukas is ranked 34 in the country in the 14s. Liz opened up with a display of unstoppable shots and Plukas missed a few too many. After jumping to a 4-0 lead, some of the big hard Plukas shots began painting the lines and Liz held on for a 6-4 win in set one. Plukas was not out there to give Liz an easy time. With her big serve she challenged Liz's return game. With Plukas serving at 1-2 in the second set, the game stretched on with Liz hitting some outstanding shots and then making some easy unforced errors when she had the advantage. This long game was the key to the set and Plukas went on to prevail in set two 6-3. Liz came out a bit shaky and emotional tight in the third set, falling to a 2-0 deficit, but she fought hard to reach some of Plukas unreachable shots and kept in the points, putting pressure on Plukas to hit better shots. Liz's tough play on every ball paid off with a nail-biting three set victory 6-4, 3-6, 6-4. This puts her in the quarterfinals against Chistine Visico from Long Beach, California.

 

     Stu and Dorothy are looking pretty happy as the sun sets in the background after watching Lizzie [play a three hour match in the middle of the day. This evening was dinner in the room with some pasta take-out from a restaurant, some Caesar salad and a bottle of wine from California. The Geyser Peak Meritage was agreeable but not very sophisticated.

 

     With only an hour's rest after her singles victory, Liz had to play doubles with her partner Alicia Menezes of Staten Island, NY. Her singles was physical and emotionally draining and the tough team of Lin Waterford and Christine Visico took the New Yorkers out 6-2, 6-2
