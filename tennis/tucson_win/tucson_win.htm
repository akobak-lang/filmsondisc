Tucson Win

December 28, 2000

Thank heaven Liz does not have an 8 am match tomorrow morning. That's because she took out the number six seed, Alessandra Jordan of Texas,  in the Girls Winter National 14s this morning in straight sets, 6-4, 6-3. Liz played a very smart match. Jordan, a ferocious counter-puncher,  tried everything in her arsenal of tricks to rattle Liz, but Kobak remained composed, playing percentage tennis, and prevailing in a brilliant last point in which Jordan got back two balls that should have been outright winners before Liz hit a down the liner forehand out of reach.

An aggressive forehand and taking ball on the rise are the key for Liz to continue her winning ways. A little bit of patience won't hurt either. Here she displays her winning form against Sara Lett in a second round win 6-3, 6-1.

 

It doesn't hurt to be comfortable. The Golf Villas at Oro Valley provides us with a nice view of the mountains from our living room balcony. It doesn't hurt that it's right across the street from the country club site where Liz's matches are now being played. A fresh tuna salad lunch and a short nap are the course of action before this afternoon's first round doubles play. Let's hope the excitement of this morning's singles victory doesn't get her too pumped.


Last night it was dinner at Anthony's in the Catalinas. A nice room with a limited menu, but a huge wine list with huge prices. After a bumbling start with champagne buckets and glasses going back and forth on a simple question, we wound up having some very nice wines. A half bottle of Billecart Salmon Rose made for mouth-puckering delight. Figs and strawberries on the palette and wonderful mousse. We followed with a 1987 Barbaresco from Bruno Giacosa. His wines are mountains of tannin and fruit and need "centuries" to open up. This may not have been the best Barbaresco year, but Giacosa's sound winemaking techniques provided us with some grand gulps. The dining was less consistent than the wine. My tuna carpaccio appetizer was superb and my lobster thermador good, but not outrageous. Lizzie did her usual Caesar salad and enjoyed some tasty squab. Dorothy had to send back some pasta that was grossly overcooked and wound up with a hastily prepared thermador that was not as good as mine. Oh, well.
